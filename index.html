Terminologies
ECHOA measurable action of personal growth or engagement within ZPLN (e.g., completing a challenge, referring a friend, posting, or sharing). Each ECHO generates personal points and qualifies the member for commissions.

Flight CrewThe total personal and team network a member builds through direct and indirect sponsorship.
True PayoutTotal member commissions (cash + tokens + bonuses + pools) divided by total company gross revenue.

COMMISSION TIMING

Weekly Period: A weekly period starts at 12:00 AM Tuesday morning and ends at 11:59:59 PM Monday night (PST).

Cutoff: All transactions, ECHO actions, and enrollments completed before the Monday cutoff count toward that week's commissions.

Processing Window: Commissions are calculated every Tuesday, finalized by Friday 11:59 PM PST.

Payout: Rewards (tokens or fiat equivalent) are distributed automatically on Saturdays.

Currency: All calculations use USD equivalents; ZPLN tokens are issued at the conversion rate at the time of payout.

PROCESS VALIDATION & AUDITING
  1. Weekly Audit Script: Validate that no payout exceeds defined caps or qualifications.
  2. Quarterly True Payout Check: Sum all rewards divided by gross revenue; trigger adjustments if above 45 percent.
  3. Smart Alerts: Notify Admin if total commission liability exceeds projected thresholds.
  4. Token Ledger Sync: Ensure crypto emissions stay within allocation limits.
NOTES FOR DEVELOPMENT TEAM
  • All payout formulas must be modular and rank-driven to allow future updates.
  • Include audit logs per transaction for compliance.
  • Ensure referral links and sponsorship trees are immutable post-enrollment.
  • Binary leg assignments can be left/right or system-assigned; maintain leg lock post-placement.
  • Include both USD and token equivalents in payout reports.
